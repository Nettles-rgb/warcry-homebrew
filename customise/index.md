# Faction Customisation

This section contains faction customisation options that don't sit neatly under other sections. These include:

* **Stormhosts**: Rules to align a Stormcast Eternals warband to a specific Stormhost.
* **Pledges to Chaos**: Rules to pledge a Slaves to Darkness warband's allegiance to a specific Chaos God.
