# Stormhosts

When selecting your roster for a **Stormcast Eternals** warband, you may optionally select a Stormhost from the following list from which your warband originates. Fighters with the Hero runemark incur an additional cost of **+10 points** if they choose a Stormhost runemark.

* Hammers of Sigmar  
* Hallowed Knights  
* Celestial Vindicators  
* Anvils of the Heldenhammer  
* Knights Excelsior  
* Celestial Warbringers  
* Tempest Lords  
* Astral Templars

Selecting a Stormhost grants an additional ability to your warband, provides access to a different battle trait (provided all fighters belong to the same Stormhost) and allows fighters in the warband to gain Stormhost-specific artefacts and traits in narrative play. 

*Designer's note: The Stormhosts provided are but a few of the famous Stormhosts from the Mortal Realms. The rules below can be used to represent the named Stormhosts, or alternatively could be used to represent other Stormhosts or your own creations with similar traits.*

## Stormhost Battle Traits

If all fighters in your warband are **Stormcast Eternals** fighters with the same **Stormhost**, when the player controlling that warband selects their battle trait, they may choose to select the corresponding battle trait from the table below.

| Stormhost | Battle Trait |
| :---- | :---- |
| Hammers of Sigmar | **First to be Forged:** Fighters with the **Hammers of Sigmar** Stormhost Runemark may make the following reaction:  <br>***\[Reaction\] First to be Forged***: *A fighter can make this reaction if it takes damage from an enemy fighter's attack action. For the remainder of the battle round, treat this fighter as 2 additional models for the purpose of controlling objectives.* |
| Hallowed Knights | **Only the Faithful:** Once per battle round, when a friendly fighter with this battle trait is selected as the target of an enemy ability, roll a dice. On a 4+, the ability has no effect. |
| Celestial Vindicators | **Driven by Vengeance:** Add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee attack actions made by friendly fighters in the same activation in which they have made a Move action. |
| Anvils of the Heldenhammer | **No True Death:** Fighters with the **Anvils of the Heldenhammer** Stormhost Runemark may make the following reaction: <br>***\[Reaction\] No True Death***: *A fighter can make this reaction after a visible friendly fighter within 6" of them is taken down. This fighter makes a bonus attack action.* |
| Knights Excelsior | **Storm of Annhilation:** Once per battle, when a friendly fighter with this battle trait takes an enemy fighter down with a melee attack action, for the rest of the battle add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee attack actions that fighter makes. |
| Celestial Warbringers | **Fearless Foresight:** After deployment, each player whose warband has this battle trait can pick a friendly fighter with this battle trait from each friendly battle group that is on the battlefield. One at a time, remove those fighters from the battlefield and pick a different battle group. Then deploy that fighter as if it were part of that battle group. |
| Tempest Lords | **Grand Strategists:** At the start of each battle round, roll a dice. On a 4+, gain 1 wild dice. |
| Astral Templars | **Beast Stalkers:** Friendly fighters with this battle trait add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee attack actions that target enemy fighters with the Monster or Beast runemark, or that have a wounds characteristic of 30 or greater. |

## Stormhost Abilities

A Stormcast Eternals fighter may use abilities with the corresponding Stormhost runemark from the below list.

| Stormhost | Runemarks | Ability |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Hammers of Sigmar  | Hero | **\[Triple\] Soul of the Stormhost**: Pick a friendly fighter that has been taken down. That fighter must have a Wounds characteristic of 22 or less and cannot have the Hero, Monster, or Mount runemarks. Set that fighter up wholly within 3" of this fighter and more than 3" from all enemy fighters. Remove a number of damage points allocated to that fighter equal to the value of this ability. |
| Hallowed Knights | Hero | **\[Triple\] Holy Crusaders**: Pick a visible friendly fighter within 6" of this fighter. That fighter makes a bonus move action. Then, if that fighter is within 1" of an enemy fighter, they make a bonus attack action. |
| Celestial Vindicators | Hero | **\[Triple\] Righteous Hatred**: Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee attack actions made by friendly fighters while they are within 6" of this fighter. |
| Anvils of the Heldenhammer | Hero | **\[Triple\] Heroes of Another Age**: Pick a visible friendly fighter within 6" of this fighter. That fighter makes a bonus attack action. |
| Knights Excelsior  | Hero | **\[Double\] No Mercy**: Pick a visible friendly fighter within 6" of this fighter. Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee attack actions made by that fighter. |
| Celestial Warbringers | Hero | **\[Double\] Astral Conjunction**: Until the end of the battle round, whenever a friendly fighter within 6" of this fighter uses an ability, add 1 to the value of that ability (to a maximum of 6). |
| Tempest Lords | Hero | **\[Triple\] Rousing Oratory:** Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to the Strength characteristic of melee attack actions made by friendly fighters while they are within 6" of this fighter. |
| Astral Templars | Hero | **\[Triple\] Cut off the Head**: Pick a visible enemy fighter with the Hero or Monster runemark within 12" of this fighter. Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of attack actions made by friendly fighters that target that enemy fighter. |

## Stormhost Artefacts

When a hero with a Stormhost runemark starts the quest “The Shattered Stormvault”, instead of selecting an artefact of power from the Stormvault Relics table, they may choose the corresponding artefact of power for their Stormhost from the below table.

| Stormhost | Artefact of Power |
| :---- | :---- |
| Hammers of Sigmar  | **God-forged Blade** <br>*Wrought by Grungni himself for the first Stormhost, this weapon strikes with explosive power.*  <br>Add 3 to the damage points allocated by critical hits from melee attack actions made by the bearer. |
| Hallowed Knights  | **Parchment of Purity** <br>*The runes etched on this honour parchment glow with the restorative power of Azyr.*  <br>At the start of each battle round, you can remove up to 3 damage points allocated to the bearer. |
| Celestial Vindicators | **Stormrage Blade** <br>*This blade stokes the rage of its bearer, driving them into such a killing frenzy they give no thought to their own defence*. <br>Add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of melee attack actions made by the bearer. Subtract 1 from the Toughness characteristic of the bearer. |
| Anvils of the Heldenhammer | **Soulthief** <br>*The Soulthief is a powerful artefact, each blow striking the victim’s very spirit.*  <br>After the bearer deals damage to an enemy fighter with a melee attack action, roll a dice. On a 2+, allocate 3 damage points to the target fighter. |
| Knights Excelsior | **Chains of Celestial Lightning** <br>*These enchanted chains can trap the unworthy in unbreakable bindings.*  <br>***\[Double\] Chains of Celestial Lightning***: *Pick a visible enemy fighter with the Hero or Monster runemark within 3" of the bearer. Until the end of the battle round, that enemy fighter cannot make move or disengage actions, and subtract 1 from the Attacks characteristic of attack actions made by that fighter (to a minimum of 1).* |
| Celestial Warbringers | **Hammers of Augury** <br>*These potent tools of divination orbit their bearer, lashing out at nearby foes.*  <br>After the bearer makes a melee attack action, if that attack action dealt damage to an enemy fighter, roll a dice. On a 2+, allocate 2 damage points to that fighter. If you allocate damage points in this way, roll another dice. On a 3+, allocate 2 additional damage points to that fighter. |
| Tempest Lords | **Patrician’s Helm** <br>*The sacred relics of the Tempest Lords are imbued with an aura of authority and command that magnifies that of the wearer.*  <br>Each time you spend a wild dice to upgrade a single, double, or triple  in the initiative phase, roll a dice. On a 5+, increase the value of the resulting double, triple or quad by one. |
| Astral Templars  | **Godbeast Plate:** <br>*This armour, quenched in the blood of the mightiest beasts, is proof against the raking claws of feral creatures.*  <br>Subtract 1 from the Strength characteristic of melee attack actions made by enemy fighters with the Monster or Beast runemark that target the bearer. |

## Stormhost Heroic Traits

When a hero with a Stormhost runemark starts the quest “Stormforged Quality”, they may choose the corresponding Heroic Trait for their Stormhost from the below table.

| Stormhost | Heroic Trait |
| :---- | :---- |
| Hammers of Sigmar | **We Cannot Fail** <br>*The Hammers of Sigmar refuse to fall until their task is completed.*  <br>Once per battle round, if a friendly fighter within 3" of this fighter is targeted by an attack action, count critical hits from that attack action as hits instead. |
| Hallowed Knights | **Martyr’s Strength** <br>*The Hallowed Knights will not fall until their foe is slain.*  <br>If this fighter is taken down by a melee attack action, roll a dice. On a 2+, before this fighter is removed from the battlefield, they make a bonus attack action. |
| Celestial Vindicators | **Single-Minded Fury** <br>*The leaders of the Celestial Vindicators are always eager to go on the offensive.*  <br>Add 2 to the damage points allocated by critical hits from attack actions made by this fighter.  |
| Anvils of the Heldenhammer | **Deathly Aura**  <br>*The leaders of the Anvils of the Heldenhammer embody their Stormhost’s sinister reputation*. <br>Enemy fighters within 3" of this fighter cannot make reactions. |
| Knights Excelsior | **Divine Executioner** <br>*Knights Excelsior commanders seek out enemy leaders with aggressive focus*. <br>Add 2 to the damage points allocated by melee attack actions made by this fighter that target a fighter with the **Hero** runemark. |
| Celestial Warbringers | **Portents and Omens** <br>*The leaders of the Celestial Warbringers receive guidance from the stars.*  <br>Once per battle round, after this fighter makes an attack action or is targeted by an attack action, you can pick one of the dice from the roll and re-roll it. |
| Tempest Lords | **Bonds of Noble Duty** <br>*When surrounded by their kin, the leaders of the Tempest Lords set a perfect martial example.*  <br>Add 2 to the Strength characteristic of melee attack actions made by this fighter while they are within 3" of at least one other friendly fighter. |
| Astral Templars | **Dauntless Hunter** <br>*The Astral Templars do not often seek permission to rush headlong at the foe, but their commanders relish giving it anyway.*  <br>After the deployment step, but before the first battle round begins, this fighter can make a bonus move action. |
