# The Anvil of Apotheosis
The Anvil of Apotheosis provides rules for creating your own unique Stormcast Eternals hero for Warcry. Due to their flexibility, these rules are not balanced for competitive play and are recommended more for narrative campaigns where everyone has created their own leader.

## Creating a Fighter
To create a custom Stormcast Eternals fighter using the Anvil of Apotheosis, follow the steps below:

### 1. Choose an Archetype
Select an **Archetype**. This determines the fighter’s Movement, Toughness and Wounds, their Runemarks, and any inherent adjustments that should be made to a fighter’s attack actions.

### 2. Choose a Chamber
Next, choose a Chamber. This determines which faction your hero belongs to, and may provide access to equipment unique to that faction.

### 3. Select Equipment
Choose the fighter’s weapons, armour, mount, and any other items from the Equipment section. Weapons provide a fighter with its attack profiles.

### 4. Choose Origin Trait
A fighter may optionally be given one Origin Trait.

### 5. Additional Runemark
A fighter may choose one additional Runemark, dependent on their Faction.

# Stormcast Eternals Anvil of Apotheosis
Each custom fighter created using the Anvil of Apotheosis represents a unique hero of your design; a single, named individual forged by the will of the gods or through the crucible of battle. This section outlines the steps of creating such an individual.

Choose one of the following chambers for your hero to belong to. The hero gains the corresponding Faction Runemark.

* Warrior Chamber
* Sacrosanct Chamber
* Vanguard Chamber
* Ruination Chamber
* Extremis Chamber
* Questor Soulsworn
* Thunderstrike Stormcasts

## 3. Equipment

Stormcast Eternals equipment falls into the following categories:

* **Wargear**: Weapons, shields, and other items of power a fighter wields in battle. A fighter may wield either **two one-handed items** or **one two-handed item** of wargear.
* **Armour Upgrades**: A fighter may have up to **one** armour upgrade.
* **Mounts**: A fighter may have up to **one** mount.
* **Miscellaneous**: Other gear that grants abilities or other upgrades. A fighter may have up to **one** item of miscellaneous gear.

## 4. Origin Trait

A fighter may optionally have up to one Origin Trait applied. 

**Champion of the People (+5pts)**  
*This fighter is a leader beloved by mortals and champions their causes.*  
If this fighter is the leader of your warband, you may include up to 250 points of Cities of Sigmar fighters in your warband as allies.

**Herald of the Storm (+5pts)**  
*This hero is talented at drawing together groups of Stormcast from different chambers to achieve more complex goals.*  
If this fighter is the leader of your warband, you may include up to 250 points of fighters from a different Stormcast Eternals faction in your warband as allies.

**Loyal Companion (+65pts)**  
*This hero was always accompanied by a faithful beast in mortal life. A gryph-hound now accompanies them everywhere, rumoured to be a reincarnation of that old companion.*  
Include one **Companion Gryph-Hound** in your warband as a fighter for no additional cost. If in narrative play this fighter is removed from your warband, their Companion Gryph-Hound is also removed from your warband.

**Tribal Warleader (+10pts)**  
*In their mortal life, this warrior rallied a dozen tribes under their banner to drive back the encroaching Chaos hordes.*  
When this fighter uses the universal **[Double] Inspiring Presence** ability, the range of that ability is increased from 6" to 9". In addition, the friendly fighter activated by that ability adds 1 to their Move characteristic until the end of their activation.

**Snatched from the Grave (+10pts)**  
*For centuries, this warrior rested as a spirit deep in Shyish, before Sigmar called them to war and clad them in flesh and lightning. Theirs is an unsettling demeanour indeed.*  
Enemy fighters within 6" of this fighter cannot use the Inspiring Presence ability or have damage points removed by any ability, battle trait, or other means.

**Redeemed (+0pts)**  
*Once in thrall to the Dark Gods, this warrior has been purified by Sigmar's Storm and is now a tireless adversary of corruption in all its forms. However, their presence draws intensified hatred from those that are still in the Dark God's grasps.*  
Add one to the damage characteristic of melee attack actions made by this fighter if the target has the Chaos Grand Alliance Runemark. Add one to the damage characteristic of melee attack actions that target this fighter if the fighter taking the action has the Chaos Grand Alliance Runemark.

**Ancestral Memory (+10pts)**  
*This fighter is always accompanied by a distant descendent, reminding them of their mortal life.*  
***Ruination Chamber*** *only.*  
This fighter starts each battle with one Memorian token.

**Fractured Soul (+10pts)**  
*The excruciating process of Reforging has taken its toll upon this warrior, and now they desperately clutch at what remains of their humanity.*  
At the end of each battle round, roll a dice for this fighter. On a 4+, allocate 2 damage points to this fighter.
While this fighter has less than half of its original wounds characteristic remaining, add 1 to the attacks characteristic of any melee attack actions made by this fighter.

**Brash and Impulsive (-10pts)**  
*When the enemy are in this warrior's sights, they show no restraint, even when it would be more tactically sound to fortify their position.*  
This fighter cannot control objectives. This fighter can make the 'Rush' universal ability as if they had spent a **[Double]** without needing or spending any ability dice, once per battle.

## 5. Additional Runemark

A fighter may choose up to one additional Runemark, at a cost of 5 points. The below runemarks can be taken for each faction:

* **Warrior Chamber**: Warrior, Brute
* **Sacrosanct Chamber**: Destroyer, Brute
* **Vanguard Chamber**: Elite, Frenzied
* **Ruination Chamber**: Terrifying, Destroyer
* **Extremis Chamber**: Destroyer, Scout, Bulwark, Elite
* **Questor Soulsworn**: Destroyer, Champion
* **Thunderstrike Stormcasts**: Ferocious, Warrior, Scout, Frenzied, Champion
