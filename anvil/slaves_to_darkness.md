# The Anvil of Apotheosis

The Anvil of Apotheosis provides rules for creating your own unique Slaves to Darkness hero for Warcry. Due to their flexibility, these rules are not balanced for competitive play and are recommended more for narrative campaigns where everyone has created their own leader. 

## Creating a Fighter

To create a custom Slaves to Darkness fighter using the Anvil of Apotheosis, follow the steps below:

### 1. Choose an Archetype

Select an **Archetype**. This determines the fighter’s **Movement**, **Toughness** and **Wounds**, their **Runemarks**, and which attack profiles it gains from equipment.

### 2. Select Equipment

Choose the fighter’s weapons, armour, mount, and any other items from the Equipment section. Weapons provide a fighter with its attack profiles.

### 3. Choose Origin Trait

A fighter may optionally be given one **Origin Trait**.

### 4. Additional Runemark

A fighter may optionally be given one additional Runemark.

# Slaves to Darkness Anvil of Apotheosis

## 2. Equipment

Slaves to Darkness equipment falls into the following categories:

* **Wargear:** Weapons, shields, and other items of power a fighter wields in battle. A fighter may wield either **two one-handed** items or **one two-handed** item of wargear.  
* **Mounts:** A fighter may have up to one **mount.**  
* **Miscellaneous:** Other gear that grants abilities or other upgrades. A fighter may have one item of **miscellaneous gear**.

### Wargear

A fighter may be equipped with two one-handed items, or one two-handed item. Weapons grant attack profiles, shown in their equipment option below.

Weapons may have multiple attack profiles. The category column indicates which Archetypes gain each profile when equipped.

### Mounts

A **Chaos Mortal** or **Chaos Sorcerer** may be equipped with one **Mount**. 
  
*Designer’s note: Like weapons, mounts are thematic but feel free to proxy them with whatever you want and choose the profile that fits your vision the best.*

## 3. Origin Trait

A fighter may optionally have up to one **Origin Trait** applied. 

**Bringer of Fire (10pts)**  
*With a blazing torch in hand, this warrior has brought the very fire of chaos to the Mortal Realms \- leaving naught but smouldering ruin in their wake.*  
This fighter may use the following ability:  
***\[Triple\] Set Ablaze:*** *Choose a terrain feature that has not yet been set ablaze within 2” of this fighter. That terrain feature is now set ablaze. This fighter counts as one additional fighter for the purposes of controlling objectives while within 6” of ablaze terrain. In addition, each time a fighter ends a move action within 1” of a terrain feature that has been set ablaze, that fighter takes one point of damage.*

**Victor of 100 Duels (10pts)**  
*This warrior has made a name for themselves for their prowess in one-on-one combat, a skilled duellist who is said to have caught the attention of the very gods.*  
If this fighter takes an enemy fighter out of action with a melee attack where no other friendly fighters are within 1\" of that fighter, and no enemy fighters are within \1" of this fighter, gain one wild dice.  If this fighter is taken out of action by an enemy fighter where no other friendly fighters are within 1\" of that fighter, and no enemy fighters are  within \1" of this fighter, your opponent gains two wild dice.

**Betrayed by the False God (10pts)**  
*Sigmar's protection was not granted to this warrior's family, yet the Dark Gods were more than willing to bestow power upon them for the vengeance they now seek.*  
Add one to the strength characteristics of melee attack actions made by this fighter if the target has the Order Grand Alliance Runemark. Add one to the strength characteristics of melee attack actions that target this fighter if the fighter making the attack actions has the Order Grand Alliance Runemark.

**Rampant Mutation (0pts)**  
*Said to have been cursed by Tzeentch, wild and uncontrolled mutations sprout from this warrior in an ever-changing flux.*  
**Cannot be mounted**  
Roll a D6 at the start of each battle round and apply the corresponding effect below to this fighter:  
1. Rotting Skin: This fighter immediately takes 3 damage. 
2. Brittle Scales: This fighter subtracts 1 from its Toughness characteristic for the first attack action that targets this fighter in this battle round. 
3. Bestial Tongue: This fighter is treated as not having the Hero runemark for the rest of the battle round. 
4. Sprouting Tentacles: Roll 2D6. The result is this fighter's move characteristic for the rest of the battle round. 
5. Extra Eyeball: Add 1 to this fighter’s strength for the rest of the battle round. 
6. Erupting Spikes: All fighters within 1” immediately takes 3 damage.

**Champion of Khorne (5)**  
*This leader’s roar is a clarion call that resonates through the Brass Citadel itself. Such is their dedication to the Eightfold Path that the frenzied mortals of the Bloodbound and Khornate daemons are drawn to their banner, eager to add to the mounting pile of skulls.*  
**Pledged to Khorne** only; see **Pledges to Chaos** for details.  
If this fighter is the leader of your warband, you may include up to 250 points of **Blades of Khorne Bloodhound or Daemons** fighters in your warband as allies. The usual maximum of three fighters with the **Hero** runemark continues to apply.

## 4. Additional Runemark

A fighter may choose up to one additional Runemark from the below list, at a cost of 5 points:

* Terrifying
* Champion
* Agile
* Elite
