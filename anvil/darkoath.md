# The Anvil of Apotheosis

The Anvil of Apotheosis provides rules for creating your own unique Darkoath Tribes hero for Warcry. Due to their flexibility, these rules are not balanced for competitive play and are recommended more for narrative campaigns where everyone has created their own leader. 

## Creating a Fighter

To create a custom Darkoath fighter using the Anvil of Apotheosis, follow the steps below:

### 1. Choose an Archetype

Select an **Archetype**. This determines the fighter’s **Movement**, **Toughness** and **Wounds**, their **Runemarks**, and which attack profiles it gains from equipment.

### 2. Choose a Faction

Next, choose a Faction. This determines your hero’s **Faction Runemark**, and in some cases alters their profile and/or the equipment and traits they have access to.

### 3. Select Equipment

Choose the fighter’s weapons, armour, mount, and any other items from the Equipment section. Weapons provide a fighter with its attack profiles.

### 4. Choose Origin Trait

A fighter may optionally be given one **Origin Trait**.

### 5. Additional Runemark

A fighter may choose one additional Runemark, dependent on their Faction.

## 3. Equipment

Darkoath equipment falls into the following categories:

* **Wargear:** Weapons, shields, and other items of power a fighter wields in battle. A fighter may wield either **two one-handed** items or **one two-handed** item of wargear.  
* **Mounts:** A fighter may have up to one **mount.**  
* **Miscellaneous:** Other gear that grants abilities or other upgrades. A fighter may have one item of **miscellaneous gear**.

### Wargear

Weapons may have multiple attack profiles. The category column indicates which Archetypes gain each profile when equipped.

At the end of the battle round, remove the token.*


## 4. Origin Trait

A fighter may optionally have up to one **Origin Trait** applied. 

**Man’s Best Friend? (80pts)**  
*Bonded in battle, this fighter is accompanied by a savage but loyal warhound.*  
Add one Chaos Warhound fighter to your roster. That fighter is classed as having the same Faction Runemark as this fighter for the purposes of determining battle traits. If in narrative play this fighter is removed from your warband roster, the war hound is also removed.

**Seer (5pts)**  
*This warrior’s eyes have been opened to the swirling currents of the aether, granting them fleeting, maddening glimpses of the future that allow them to twist fate to their own advantage.*  
This fighter may use the following ability:  
***\[Double\] Visions of Glory:*** *Roll a number of dice equal to the value of this ability. For each roll of 5+, add one wild dice to your saved wild dice*

**Bestial Spirit (5pts)**  
*The barrier between man and beast wears thin for this warrior, whose soul echoes with the feral hunger of wild spirits of Ghur.*  
This fighter may use the following ability:  
***\[Double\] Unleash:*** *Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to this fighter's Move characteristic, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee attack actions made by this fighter, and this fighter has the* **Beast** *runemark.*

## 5. Additional Runemark

A fighter may choose up to one additional Runemark, at a cost of 5 points. The below runemarks can be taken for each faction:

* **Darkoath:** Ferocious, Destroyer, Bulwark, Scout, Berserker, Elite, Brute  
* **Chaos Legionnaires:** Brute, Berserker (only if equipped with a **Darkiron Spear**).  
* **Claws of Karanak**: Terrifying  
* **Corvus Cabal:** Agile, Scout, Champion  
* **Cypher Lords:** Destroyer (only if equipped with a melee weapon with a range of 2 or more).  
* **Darkoath Savagers:** Berserker  
* **The Horns of Hashut:** Brute, Warrior  
* **Iron Golem:** Brute, Destroyer (Darkoath Brute only)  
* **The Jade Obelisk:** Icon Bearer (Darkoath Chieftain and Brute only), Warrior, Destroyer  
* **Rotmire Creed:** Brute  
* **Scions of the Flame:** Brute, Destroyer  
* **Spire Tyrants:** Brute, Warrior  
* **Splintered Fang**: Mystic, Brute  
* **Tarantulas Brood:** Agile, Scout  
* **The Unmade:** Destroyer, Warrior
