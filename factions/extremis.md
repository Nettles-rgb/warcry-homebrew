# Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber

*Many are the ways that the godbeast Dracothion has contributed to Sigmar’s crusade, but that alliance is embodied most of all in the drake-riding knights of the Chambers Extremis. The combination of the sons of Sigmar and the star-scaled beasts of the Great Drake has proven an unstoppable force upon many a battlefield.*

### Battle Trait

| Runemark | Trait |
| :---- | :---- |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber | **Dracoth Riders:** Friendly fighters with the **mounted** runemark may make climb actions. |

### Reaction

| Runemark | Reaction |
| :---- | :---- |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber | **Living Weapon:** A fighter can make this reaction after they are targeted by a melee attack action but before the hit rolls are made. For each hit roll from that attack action that misses, allocate 1 damage point to the attacking fighter. This reaction cannot be made by fighters with the **Beast** runemark. |

### Abilities

| Runemark | Reaction |
| :---- | :---- |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber, Elite | **\[Double\] Cleaving Blow**: Until the end of this fighter's activation, add 1 to the Attacks and Strength characteristics of melee attack actions made by this fighter that target an enemy fighter with a Wounds characteristic of 15 or more. |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber, Destroyer | **\[Double\] Thunderstruck**: Until the end of the battle round, enemy fighters within 3" of this fighter cannot make reactions. |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber, Scout | **\[Triple\] Overwhelming Volleystorm**: Until the end of this fighter's activation, if a ranged attack action made by this fighter scores any hits or critical hits, subtract 1 from the target fighter's Toughness characteristic (to a minimum of 1\) until the end of the battle round. |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber, Bulwark | **\[Triple\] Impaling Strikes**: Until the end of this fighter's activation, the next time this fighter finishes a move action within 1" of an enemy fighter, pick a visible enemy fighter within 1" of this fighter. Allocate a number of damage points to that fighter equal to the value of this ability. |
| Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber, Hero | **\[Triple\] Righteous Avengers**: Pick a visible friendly fighter within 6" of this fighter that has not activated yet this battle round. You can activate that fighter immediately after this fighter's activation ends. That fighter adds 1 to the strength characteristics of any attack actions it makes until the end of its activation. |
