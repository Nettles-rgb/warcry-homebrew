# Abilities

Add the following abilities to the Stormcast Eternals: Thunderstrike Stormcast faction:

| Runemark | Ability |
| :---: | :---: |
| Hero, Destroyer | **[Triple] Furious Retribution:** Pick a visible friendly fighter within 6" of this fighter that has not activated yet this battle round. You can activate that fighter immediately after this fighter's activation ends. That fighter adds 1 to the strength characteristics of any attack actions it makes until the end of its activation. |
| Hero, Terrifying | **[Triple] Lightning Storm:** Pick a point on the battlefield within 12" of this fighter and roll a number of dice equal to the value of this ability. For each roll of 4+, allocate 1 damage point to all enemy fighters within 4” of the selected point. |
