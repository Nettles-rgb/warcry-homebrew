# Barely Battle Ready's Warcry Homebrew Compendium

Welcome to my collection of Warcry homebrew rules. Much like my painting, they're barely battle-ready. In other words, wildly untested expressions of my need to output homebrew rules for any game I'm interested in.

Use the sidebar navigation to browse custom factions, narrative campaign quests, Anvils of Apotheosis and other rules.
