# Warbands of Renown

Warbands of Renown are remixed factions that either apply a unique spin on a faction with limitations in roster selection, or give you the opportunity to mix factions together in ways that ally rules wouldn't normally support.

Fighters from a Warband of Renown may **never** be taken as allies in another warband.
