# Stormcast Quests

The following quests can be undertaken by the following warbands:

* Stormcast Eternals: Thunderstrike Stormcast,
* Stormcast Eternals: Warrior Chamber, 
* Stormcast Eternals: Sacrosanct Chamber,   
* Stormcast Eternals: Vanguard Chamber,  
* Stormcast Eternals: Questor Soulsworn,   
* Stormcast Eternals: Ruination Chamber,   
* Stormcast Eternals: Extremis Chamber

For homebrew rules purposes they are collectively referred to as **Stormcast Eternals warbands**. 

## The Shattered Stormvault

*Whispers reach your warband of an ancient Stormvault, cracked open by the Gnarlwood’s shifting roots. Within are relics blessed by the Six Smiths, intended for the God-King’s armies but lost to the Era of the Beast. You must locate this cache and reclaim these holy relics before they fall into the hands of the God-King’s enemies.*

Pick 1 artefact of power from the Stormvault Relics table (see overleaf) or, if applicable, the artefact of power that is unique to your warband's Stormhost (see *Stormhosts*) and note it in your quest log. You cannot pick an artefact of power already borne by a fighter in your warband.

While embarked upon this quest, you must keep track of the progress your warband makes, represented by a discovery score. 

In step 4 of the aftermath phase of a campaign battle, add 1 to your score. Add a further 1 to your score:

* If your warband won the battle;  
* If your warband controls more objectives than your opponent;  
* If a friendly fighter is carrying treasure at the end of the battle. 

You can complete this quest once your discovery score is 6 or more. When you complete this quest, you obtain the artefact of power that was noted in your quest log.

## Stormforged Quality

*Since their forging, the warriors of the Stormhosts have died a hundred deaths. Yet, for some, the Reforging takes a toll that requires a renewal of purpose. Your leader seeks to prove their worthiness to Sigmar once more, purging the Gnarlwood of Sigmar’s enemies to prove their quality anew.*

Pick a fighter with the Hero runemark in your warband that can gain a heroic trait and note them in your quest log.

While embarked upon this quest, you must keep track of your Warband's efforts to achieve their task with a Quality score.

In step 4 of the aftermath phase of a campaign battle, add 1 to your score. Add a further 1 to your score:

* If your warband won the battle;  
* If a fighter with the Hero, Monster or Terrifying Runemark, or with 25 or more wounds, that was taken down by an attack action or ability from the fighter undertaking this quest.

You can complete this quest in step 4 of the aftermath sequence if your Quality score is 6 or more. If you do so, you can give the fighter you picked when you embarked on this quest one of the heroic traits from the Champion of Azyr table overleaf, or alternatively the heroic trait unique to  their Stormhost (see *Stormhosts*).
