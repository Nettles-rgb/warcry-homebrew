# Slaves to Darkness Quests

The following quests can be undertaken by Slaves to Darkness warbands. 

## The Shadow of the Varanspire

*For the Slaves to Darkness, the ultimate ambition is not merely survival, but recognition by the Everchosen \- at least the one they venerate. Your warband seeks to carve a path of slaughter so distinct that the gates of the Varanspire will open to you.*

Pick 1 artefact of power from the Gifts of Chaos table (see below) or, if applicable, the artefact of power that is unique to your warband's Pledge to Chaos and note it in your quest log. You cannot pick an artefact of power already borne by a fighter in your warband.

While embarked upon this quest, you must keep track of the progress your warband makes, represented by a favour score. In step 4 of the aftermath phase, increase your discovery score as follows:

* Add 2 to your Favor Score if you won the battle.  
* Add 1 to your Favor Score if your Leader took down an enemy fighter with the Hero runemark.

In addition, you can pick 1 fighter from your warband and send them forth to pillage in the name of the Everchosen. To do so, roll a dice and add the score rolled to your progress score. In addition, on a 1, the fighter being rolled for is attacked by something within the Gnarlwood. Make an injury roll for that fighter.

You can complete this quest once your favour score is 8 or more. When you complete this quest, you obtain the artefact of power that was noted in your quest log.

## Ritual of the Eight Points

*The ruinous powers demand more than blood; they demand souls. Your warband has discovered a site of ancient power where a ritual can be performed to imbue your champion with the essence of the Dark Gods.*

Pick a fighter with the Hero runemark in your warband that can gain a heroic trait and note them in your quest log.

While embarked upon this quest, you must keep track of your Warband's efforts to achieve their task with a Soul Tally.

Each time the Aspirant takes down an enemy fighter, add 1 to your Soul Tally. If the enemy fighter had the Hero or Monster runemark, add 3 instead.

You can complete this quest in step 4 of the aftermath sequence if your Soul Tally is 15 or more. If you do so, you can give the fighter you picked when you embarked on this quest one of the heroic traits from the Dark Essence table below.
